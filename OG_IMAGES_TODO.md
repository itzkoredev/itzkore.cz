## Design Tools

**Recommended:**
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Easy online editor with templates
- [Figma](https://www.figma.com/) - Professional design tool
- Photoshop/GIMP - Advanced editing

**Templates:**
- Search for "Open Graph template 1200x630"
- Use dark background (#0a0a0a or #1a1a1a)
- High contrast text (white or brand colors)
- Minimal text, bold typography
